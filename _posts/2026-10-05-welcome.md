Hi all, it's me, SP4R0W. This website has existed for <i>quite</i> some time now, hasn't it? Well, it did, although it never had any content, besides the [About](https://sparrowworks.github.io/about/) page. But guess what - it has now changed! We welcome you to our updated website!

## What's this? Website content?
Yes! Now you can see all of our released games here on the webpage! There's also a short description plus some screenshots so you know what you're looking at. Each game also includes a widget that will let you play and/or download the game on its respective itch.io page. You might say this website finally has a least <b>some</b> purpose.

## Okay... is that it?
Not exactly. Previously our website was just a blatant copy of the [Chirpy](https://chirpy.cotes.page/) theme with no changes made whatsoever. Thankfully, those times are gone, and we have made some small changes to it. The list includes (but is not limited to):
<br>
- Changes to our social button on the bottom-left side of the page. The broken feed button is now gone! (This is actually a bug coming from the original 😅)
- New pages! Gone is the default <i>Categories</i> page - welcome <i>Games</i> and <i>Posts</i>! You can probably guess what they stand for, but they separate the two main categories so you don't have to scroll through the Home page to find what you're looking for. We hope this change will bring some QoL to this website.
- The favicon is now actually our logo! This was the standard Chirpy ant before, which is really embarassing when you have a foreign logo on your website 😬
- We also refreshed our [About](https://sparrowworks.github.io/about/) page so it's a bit more serious and in-tone.

## Does this mean...
So you might be wondering - does the sudden website update mean anything? Like, are you going to make new games (maybe a sequel... 👀)? I would love to say yes, but honestly we do not want to promise you anything at this point. We know that we have had a few of our fans asking for any updates, and for that we are very thankful. Your support means a lot to us, and we are very happy to have you here! However, we also do realise that we have promised new content for our games, including Infamy, and we haven't delivered on that, which we're very sorry for. It's not all gone, and we are working on <i>*something*</i>, that we can't share yet. Stay tuned for more!

### Thank you for reading this short update post, hopefully with the rebirth of our website we will be posting more of these! See you next time!
