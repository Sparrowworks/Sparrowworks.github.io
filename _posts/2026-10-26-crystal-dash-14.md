Hello, we are pleased to announce that a new update for Crystal Dash has just arrived! Version 1.4 brings a much-needed revamp of the game, coming in with new graphical assets, new music and more! Let's go through everything step by step:

## New graphical assets
Our first (and most noticeable) change is the new graphical assets! We have replaced the old gems with brand new ones! Now each gem has a unique shape, in addition to its unique color. This should hopefully make it easier to play for people with colorblindness.
<br>
You may also notice new UI and new Font. This has been changed so that the game is easier to play on mobile devices. The new buttons should hopefully help with that. We hope you will enjoy the new looks!

## New sounds and music
Next change on our list is new audio! We have decided to combine both PC and Web soundtracks from the previous version - v1.3 - and threw in some new tracks as well! We have also replaced all the sound effects, to hopefully fit better with the game's overall atmosphere.
<br>
<i>P.S.: Your ears will no longer hurt when first launching the game, as we have lowered the base volume of all the sounds in the game! You're welcome!</i>

## Online Leaderboards
Yes, you saw it, we have added an Online Leaderboard support for Crystal Dash! You can now compete with others for the top spot on the leaderboard! There are 10 places, so you will have to do your best in order to get yourself a place there. During gameplay, you can also see what is your current place, and how close you are to the overtaking the next one.

## Faster loading times
We have also took some time, and made sure the game loads as fast as it is possible. Sadly, Godot isn't the best choice when it comes to web games, but we think we did our best with this one. We hope you'll notice the improvements!
