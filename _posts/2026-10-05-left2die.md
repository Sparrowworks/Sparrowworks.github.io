## Game Description

This is a 2D, top-down zombie coop shooter game. Gather up to 4 players, and defend yourselves against hordes of zombies. Can you reach the highest score before your inevitable death?
<br>
<br>
To play this game, download the .zip file and extract the contents. You will need to setup a LAN server in order to play (you can use Hamachi or RadminVPN as an example).
