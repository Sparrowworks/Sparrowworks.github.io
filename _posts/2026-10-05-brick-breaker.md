## Game Description

Welcome to Brick Breaker - a clone of one of the most beloved games ever - Arkanoid! Move the paddle to break bricks and don't let the ball fall into the pit! Finish all 10 levels or create your own with the Level Editor. Note that saving levels to your computer is available only in the Desktop version. HTML build only supports testing levels directly from the editor.
