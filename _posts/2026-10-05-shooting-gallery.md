## Game Description

Relax and enjoy yourself with Shooting Gallery - and point and shoot game, where your goal is to shoot down as many targets as possible. Clear out waves of targets and get the highest score. Upgrade your weapon from cash earned by finishing levels. There's also a Time Attack mode, where your objective is to get the required score as fast as possible. Can you get a Platinum rank in every level on each mode? Good luck!
