## Our Privacy Policy

Sparrowworks ("we," "our," or "us") provides this Privacy Policy to explain how we collect, use, and disclose information when using our products (the "Games") and/or our websites. By using them, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. This Privacy Policy does not apply to and cannot control activities when you click a link to other sites from a Sparrowworks websites or product. We highly recommend that you read the privacy policy of any third-party website you may be directed to before providing any personally identifiable information. We keep direct data collection to a minimum. Depending on how you interact with the Game, the following data may be processed:

### 1. Information We Collect:
Depending on how you interact with the Game, the following data may be processed:

- Leaderboard Username: If you choose to submit your high score to our public online leaderboard, you will be prompted to enter a player name or alias ("Username"). We recommend that the player does not use their real name, email address, or any personally identifiable information as their username, as leaderboards are publicly visible to other players.

### 2. Information Collected Automatically:
- Cookies and/or Local Storage: We use standard web technologies to operate the Game, save your progress, and understand how players interact with our websites and/or products. We do not use these technologies to personally identify you outside of our Game. This includes your browser’s local storage technologies (such as localStorage, sessionStorage, or IndexedDB) solely to deliver essential gameplay functionality. This data is stored directly on your device and may include:
    - Game Progress & High Scores: Saving your level completion, unlocked items, and personal best scores so you do not lose progress between sessions.
    - Player Settings: Remembering your preferences, such as volume levels, display settings, and control bindings.
    - Cached Credentials: Storing your chosen leaderboard alias locally for convenience when submitting scores. You can clear your local storage at any time via your browser settings, though doing so will reset your saved game progress and preferences.
- Third-Party Analytics Cookies (Google Analytics): We use Google Analytics to collect aggregated, non-personally identifiable information about how visitors find and play our Games and/or use our websites. You can prevent Google Analytics from recognizing you on return visits by disabling cookies in your browser settings.

### 3. Third-Party Services We Use:
We do not operate our own database servers for leaderboards, ad delivery and analyitcs. Instead, we rely on trusted third-party service providers:
- Leaderboard & Backend Services (Talo): We use Talo to manage and host our online leaderboards, Usernames submitted by players, score tracking, and transient technical identifiers generated by the service. Please review the [Talo Privacy Policy](https://trytalo.com/privacy).
- Advertising (GameMonetize SDK): We use the GameMonetize SDK to serve banner and/or video advertisements within the Game. Please review the [GameMonetize Privacy Policy](https://gamemonetize.com/privacypolicy).

Disclaimer: External links to third-party policies are provided for convenience; we do not control, guarantee, or assume responsibility for broken links, URL changes, or updates to third-party terms.

### 4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K):
We do not knowingly collect personal data directly from children. If you believe a child under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area) has provided personal information via our leaderboard, please contact us immediately at <b>sparrowworks@proton.me</b>, and we will take steps to remove that information from our records and third-party databases.

### 5. Your Data Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA):
Depending on your geographic location, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including:
- The Right to Access: Request details on what data is held about you.
- The Right to Deletion: Request the deletion of your leaderboard username and score.
- The Right to Opt Out: Opt out of personalized advertising via your browser's cookie settings or industry opt-out tools (e.g., Network Advertising Initiative or Your Online Choices).

To request the removal of your username from the leaderboard, please contact us at <b>sparrowworks@proton.me</b>.

If you have questions, feedback, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
- Email: <b>sparrowworks@proton.me</b>
- [Our Socials](https://linktr.ee/sparrowworks)

### 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our Game features or legal requirements along with a "Last Updated" date.
